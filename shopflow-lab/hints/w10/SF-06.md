# SF-06 hints: pricing quote

<details><summary>Level 1</summary>

Do the pieces one at a time: first `/api/shop`, then the quote with one item and country `US`, then taxes, then shipping, then the error cases.
</details>

<details><summary>Level 2</summary>

`PricingService` needs three collaborators: `PriceList`, `TaxCalculator`, `ShippingPolicy`. How do they get into the class? (EX-10-01 showed you.)
</details>

<details><summary>Level 3</summary>

If the application context fails with "No qualifying bean of type ShippingPolicy", you have not finished EX-10-03. Run `Ex3ShippingProfilesTest` first.
</details>

<details><summary>Level 4</summary>

Money rules: add with `BigDecimal.add`, multiply with `BigDecimal.valueOf(quantity)`, keep scale 2 with `setScale(2)`. For unknown SKUs use `Optional.orElseThrow(() -> new UnknownSkuException(...))`. For the 400 on bad input look at `@Valid` on the controller parameter and at `@ExceptionHandler`.
</details>

<details><summary>Level 5</summary>

subtotal = sum(price x qty); tax = taxCalculator.taxFor(subtotal, country); shipping = shippingPolicy.costFor(subtotal); total = subtotal + tax + shipping. Controller: `@Valid @RequestBody`, delegate to the service, and an `@ExceptionHandler(UnknownSkuException.class)` returning `ProblemDetail.forStatusAndDetail(HttpStatus.BAD_REQUEST, ex.getMessage())`.
</details>
