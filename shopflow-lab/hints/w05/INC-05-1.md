# INC-05-1: loyalty points vanish after a rename

Open one level at a time, and only after you have tried for about 10 minutes at the current level.

<details><summary>Level 1: the symptom, restated</summary>

The points are not lost: `totalPoints()` still shows them. They just cannot be found by `pointsOf(customer)` after `rename`.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 2: where to look</summary>

Look at what `LoyaltyLedger` uses as the `HashMap` key, and at how that class computes `equals` and `hashCode`.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 3: which tool to use</summary>

Use the debugger: put a breakpoint in `pointsOf`, and evaluate `customer.hashCode()` before and after `rename`. Then look inside the map's `table` field to see which bucket the entry is in.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 4: the concept to search for</summary>

Search: "mutable HashMap key hashCode changes" and Effective Java Item 11 (always override hashCode when you override equals) plus the Javadoc contract of `Object.hashCode`: it must be consistent while the fields used in `equals` do not change.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 5: the shape of the fix</summary>

Which fields should define who a customer *is*? Decide, then make `equals` and `hashCode` use only the fields that can never change. (Alternative: key the map by the immutable id instead of by the object.)

</details>
