# INC-05-2: gift cubes are charged for the wrong volume

Open one level at a time, and only after you have tried for about 10 minutes at the current level.

<details><summary>Level 1: the symptom, restated</summary>

After resizing a cube to 10x20x30 the volume is 27000 instead of 6000, and the box you passed in has been changed.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 2: where to look</summary>

Read `Packaging.resized`, then the nested `Cube` class in `Boxes.java`. What does each setter do on a cube?

</details>

<details><summary>Level 3: which tool to use</summary>

Write down, on paper, the three setter calls and the state of the three fields after each call, for a `Cube`. Or step through the first test with the debugger.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 4: the concept to search for</summary>

Search: "Liskov substitution principle" and "Square Rectangle problem". Read Effective Java Item 18 (favor composition over inheritance) and Item 17 (minimize mutability).

</details>

<details><summary>Level 5: the shape of the fix</summary>

The subclass breaks an assumption its parent's callers rely on ("setting the width changes only the width"). Remove the problem at the root: make `Box` immutable, have `resized` return a new box, and drop the `Cube` subclass: a cube is just a box whose sides happen to be equal.

</details>
