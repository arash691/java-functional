# SF-03: the Order lifecycle

Open one level at a time, and only after you have tried for about 10 minutes at the current level.

<details><summary>Level 1: the symptom, restated</summary>

Most `OrderTest` failures say `UnsupportedOperationException: TODO SF-03`: the methods are stubs.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 2: where to look</summary>

Start with the smallest test: `new Order("O-1")` is fine already. The next smallest is `approvedMovesToPaidAndKeepsTransactionId`. Make that one green before looking at the others.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 3: which tool to use</summary>

Write one private method `transitionTo(OrderStatus next)` that does the legal-move check and the history entry. Every public method should be one or two lines that call it.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 4: the concept to search for</summary>

Search: "Java switch pattern matching sealed interface" (JEP 441) for `applyPayment`. You do not need a `default` branch for a sealed type; the compiler checks you covered every case.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 5: the shape of the fix</summary>

The message `Cannot go from X to Y` comes from `transitionTo`. Payments on a non-CREATED order are rejected *before* the switch. Declined only increments the counter; Pending does nothing.

</details>
