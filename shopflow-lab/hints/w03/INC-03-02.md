# INC-03-02: negative order total (RL-02)

Open **one level at a time**, and only after you have tried for about 10 minutes at the previous level.
Write down what you tried before opening the next level.

<details><summary>Level 1: the symptom, restated</summary>

A bulk order of 10,000 units at 2,500.00 produces a negative total. Small orders are fine.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 2: where to look</summary>

Look at `BulkOrder.totalCents` and `sumCents`. What is the largest value an `int` can hold, and what is 250,000 * 10,000?

</details>

<details><summary>Level 3: which tool to use</summary>

In a scratch `main` print `Integer.MAX_VALUE`, `Integer.MAX_VALUE + 1`, and `250_000 * 10_000`. Then print the same computation as a `long`.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 4: the concept to search for</summary>

Search for: *Java integer overflow*, *two's complement wraparound*, *Math.multiplyExact*, *Math.addExact*. Read the Javadoc of `Math.multiplyExact(int,int)`.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 5: the shape of the fix (try to write it yourself first)</summary>

Use the exact arithmetic methods so an overflow fails loudly instead of wrapping silently. The tests expect `ArithmeticException`. (Later weeks will show a better fix: a type that cannot overflow for money.)

</details>
