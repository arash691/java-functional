# SF-02: Money (hardest part: rounding and equality)

Open **one level at a time**, and only after you have tried for about 10 minutes at the previous level.
Write down what you tried before opening the next level.

<details><summary>Level 1: the symptom, restated</summary>

Tests about rounding or equality fail even though the arithmetic looks right.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 2: where to look</summary>

Two classic traps: `new BigDecimal(double)` and `BigDecimal.equals` comparing the scale too (`1.5` is not equal to `1.50`).

</details>

<details><summary>Level 3: which tool to use</summary>

Print `new BigDecimal(2.675)` and `new BigDecimal("2.675")`. Print `new BigDecimal("1.5").equals(new BigDecimal("1.50"))` and `compareTo`.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 4: the concept to search for</summary>

Search for: *BigDecimal setScale RoundingMode HALF_EVEN banker's rounding*, *BigDecimal equals vs compareTo*, *Martin Fowler Money pattern*.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 5: the shape of the fix (try to write it yourself first)</summary>

Normalize in the constructor: `amount.setScale(2, RoundingMode.HALF_EVEN)`. Then `equals` can compare the normalized `BigDecimal` and the currency. Parse from `String`, never from `double`.

</details>
