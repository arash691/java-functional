# EX-11-03 hints: validation and a useful 400

<details><summary>Level 1</summary>

The request is accepted even when the data is nonsense, because nothing checks it. Two things are missing: the rules, and something that applies them.
</details>

<details><summary>Level 2</summary>

Rules live on the fields of `ProductRequest` as Jakarta Bean Validation annotations. They are applied when the controller parameter is marked `@Valid`.
</details>

<details><summary>Level 3</summary>

Useful annotations: `@NotBlank`, `@NotNull`, `@Pattern(regexp=...)`, `@Size(max=...)`, `@Positive`, `@Digits(integer=..., fraction=...)`.
</details>

<details><summary>Level 4</summary>

Once validation runs, Spring already answers 400 with a `ProblemDetail`, but without the list of bad fields. `ApiExceptionHandler` extends `ResponseEntityExceptionHandler`: override `handleMethodArgumentNotValid` and add a property `errors` (a list of `{field, message}`) to the `ProblemDetail` you get from `ex.getBody()`.
</details>

<details><summary>Level 5</summary>

`ex.getBindingResult().getFieldErrors()` gives `getField()` and `getDefaultMessage()`. Build a `List<Map<String,String>>`, call `problem.setProperty("errors", list)`, then `return handleExceptionInternal(ex, problem, headers, status, request);`.
</details>
