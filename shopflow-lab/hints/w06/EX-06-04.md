# EX-06-04: generics and PECS

Open one level at a time, and only after you have tried for about 10 minutes at the current level.

<details><summary>Level 1: the symptom, restated</summary>

The three methods only throw `UnsupportedOperationException`, and the tests also check the *signatures*: they must compile for `LocalDate` and `List<Number>`.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 2: where to look</summary>

Do `keyOfMaxValue` first (no wildcards in the parameter), then `maxOf`, then `copyAll`.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 3: which tool to use</summary>

If the tests do not compile, read the compiler message: it names the type it could not infer. Hover over the call in the IDE to see the inferred `T`.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 4: the concept to search for</summary>

Search: "PECS producer extends consumer super" and Effective Java Item 31. For `maxOf`, ask: why `Comparable<? super T>` and not `Comparable<T>`? (Hint: `LocalDate` implements `Comparable<ChronoLocalDate>`.)

</details>

<details><summary>Level 5: the shape of the fix</summary>

`maxOf`: take an iterator, throw `NoSuchElementException` if empty, keep the best seen so far with `compareTo`. `copyAll`: loop over `src` (a producer: `? extends T`) and `add` to `dst` (a consumer: `? super T`). The signatures are already given; do not change them.

</details>
