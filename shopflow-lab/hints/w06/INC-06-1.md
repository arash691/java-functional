# INC-06-1: the nightly promotion cleanup crashes

Open one level at a time, and only after you have tried for about 10 minutes at the current level.

<details><summary>Level 1: the symptom, restated</summary>

The job throws `ConcurrentModificationException`. In one input shape it does not throw but leaves an expired promotion behind.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 2: where to look</summary>

Read `PromotionCleanup.removeExpired`. What is happening to the list while the `for` loop walks over it?

</details>

<details><summary>Level 3: which tool to use</summary>

Read the stack trace to the line inside `ArrayList$Itr`. Then use the debugger on the `secondToLastQuirk` test: step through the loop and watch the list size and the iterator's cursor.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 4: the concept to search for</summary>

Search: "ConcurrentModificationException modCount fail-fast iterator", and "ArrayList iterator hasNext cursor != size" to see why removing the second-to-last element ends the loop early without an exception.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 5: the shape of the fix</summary>

Do not remove from a collection while a for-each iterates it. Use `List.removeIf` (or `Iterator.remove()`), and compute the number removed from the size before and after.

</details>
