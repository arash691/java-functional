# INC-06-2: the optimised report got slower

Open one level at a time, and only after you have tried for about 10 minutes at the current level.

<details><summary>Level 1: the symptom, restated</summary>

Summing 60,000 amounts takes over a second; it used to be instant.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 2: where to look</summary>

Compare how `Reports.newAmountsList()` creates the list with how `sumAll` reads it.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 3: which tool to use</summary>

Run `ComplexityLab` (the worked example) and compare `get(i)` on an `ArrayList` and on a `LinkedList`. Or take two thread dumps while the test runs and look at the top frame.

</details>

<details><summary>Level 4: the concept to search for</summary>

Search: "LinkedList get(index) complexity" and "ArrayList vs LinkedList". Read the Javadoc of `LinkedList.get`: what must it do to find element 30,000?

</details>

<details><summary>Level 5: the shape of the fix</summary>

A loop with `get(i)` over a `LinkedList` is O(n²). Two valid fixes: return an `ArrayList` from `newAmountsList()`, or iterate with for-each so the list's own iterator is used. Prefer the first unless you truly need cheap inserts in the middle.

</details>
